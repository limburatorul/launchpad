## 1.2 | 2026-10-26 | Counted, nothing more
- When Launchpad downloads an app, it tells the site which one and how (installed, updated, trial or bought), so we can see how many people get our apps through Launchpad. It's the same anonymous count the site keeps for its own download buttons: no account, no address, nothing about your PC.
- **1.2.1:** the pictures at the top of these notes for 1.1 and 1.2 were blank; now they show.

## 1.1 | 2026-10-26 | Your paid apps update from here too
Launchpad now knows which of your apps you've bought, and installs their updates with the rest.
**Bought apps, updated in the same go**
- **Update** on a paid app you own downloads its new version with your purchase and installs it alongside the free ones, still with a single admin prompt. Each download is checked against the checksum published for it.
- Your apps share your purchase with Launchpad (and Launchpad with them), so you type the link from your purchase email once at most, and never twice.
- Shelf updates from here while your Update Pass covers the new version; past it, Shelf shows how to renew.
**Trials**
- A running trial updates to the newest trial. When a trial has ended, its card says so and offers it with 20% off instead.

## 1.0 | 2026-10-26 | Every app in one place
Launchpad shows every Protagonist Labs app on one screen: the ones you have, the ones with something new, and the rest, one click away.
**What you have, and what's new**
- Your apps with their real icons and the version you're on. When a new version is out, the card says so and a banner at the top sums it up.
- **Update all** installs the free apps' new versions in one go, with a single admin prompt for the lot. Every download is checked against the checksum published with it before it runs.
- Paid apps keep updating themselves, with your purchase: their **Update** button opens them and they take it from there.
**The rest, one click away**
- Free apps install with **Install**. Paid apps start their 14-day trial with **Try free**.
- A trial shows how many days are left on its card. Three days before it ends, and again on its last day, Launchpad lets you know, with the 20% off that comes with every trial.
**Quietly in the corner**
- Launchpad starts with Windows in the notification area and looks for new versions every 6 hours. Its notices are its own, in its colours, and wait while you're playing full screen.
- Every app still updates itself on its own too: Launchpad only brings them together.
